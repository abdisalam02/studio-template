## Asset Compliance Rules
1. **Forbidden Sources:** Pinterest, Instagram, Google Images, TikTok, or hotlinked third-party image hosts.
2. **Client Content:** The client must provide written confirmation that they own or hold licensing for all uploaded images and logos.
3. **Attribution:** If any CC-BY or stock imagery is used, visible attribution must be retained.
